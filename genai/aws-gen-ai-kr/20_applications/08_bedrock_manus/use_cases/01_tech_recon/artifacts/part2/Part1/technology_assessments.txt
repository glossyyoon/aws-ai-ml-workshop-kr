SCORING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

IMPACT SCORING (1-9 Scale):
  • 7-9 (Transformative): Revolutionary impact on pharmaceutical operations
  • 4-6 (Significant): Meaningful improvements to specific processes
  • 1-3 (Incremental): Limited or niche applications

MATURITY SCORING (1-9 Scale):
  • 7-9 (Deployment): Production-ready, commercially available
  • 4-6 (Development): Pilot/testing phase, proven concepts
  • 1-3 (Conceptual): Early research, experimental stage

MOMENTUM SCORING (1-9 Scale):
  • 7-9 (Accelerating): Rapid growth, increasing investment
  • 4-6 (Stable): Steady growth, consistent adoption
  • 1-3 (Slowing): Declining interest or stagnant growth

COMPOSITE SCORE CALCULATION:
  Composite = (Impact × 0.4) + (Maturity × 0.4) + (Momentum × 0.2)
